Eight Leaves Style - Shizuna's moveset for Estelle
Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter (Steam)

Estelle learns "Eight Leaves Style" (level 38): a 0 CP craft that has her draw a katana and fight with
Shizuna's moves from Trails beyond the Horizon. "Cassius Style" switches back to her staff. If she is
already level 38 or higher, she learns it at the start of your next battle.

  - Shizuna's idle, movement, attacks and follow-ups, with her katana and sheath
  - Crafts: Duality, Raging Blizzard, Ninety-Nine Gales, Oni-Kagura, Zeroth Gleam (S-craft)
  - Spirit Unification (100 CP): STR up, SPD up and Foresight for 4 turns, gold eyes and an aura, and the
    Spirit Unification versions of her crafts until she switches back, is knocked out or the battle ends
  - Estelle's own voice lines throughout; her staff for the 3 BP chain attacks

REQUIREMENTS
  - Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter, with its original game files
  - Trails beyond the Horizon, installed (the mod's motions, katana and effects are rebuilt from it;
    none of Horizon's files are included in this download)
  - About 30 MB of free space on the drive with Trails in the Sky

INSTALL
  1. Close the game.
  2. Run EightLeavesStyleInstaller.exe.
  3. Check the two folders (filled in from Steam if found), then press Install.
     It takes under a minute. The installer checks every rebuilt file against the mod's own build.

UNINSTALL
  Run the installer and press Uninstall: the game's archives are restored exactly as they were.
  Uninstall before updating the game or verifying its files; after a game update, install again.

NOTES
  - The installer adds the mod's files to the game's archives in pac\steam and keeps what it needs to
    undo that in pac\steam\eight_leaves_style_undo. Don't delete that folder while the mod is installed.
  - If the installer says game files are not the versions the mod was made for, the game was updated or
    another mod changed the same files. Use Steam > Properties > Installed Files > Verify integrity, then
    install again.
  - Other mods that change Estelle's battle script or the craft tables are not compatible.
  - Untested: loading a save made with the mod after uninstalling it. Such a save lists Eight Leaves Style
    among Estelle's crafts, which the game's own tables don't have. Keep a save from before installing.
